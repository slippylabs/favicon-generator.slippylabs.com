FAVICON GENERATOR

Turn any image into a multi-size favicon.ico, written byte by byte in your browser. Pick the sizes you want, check the previews at actual pixel size, and download.

The .ico container is written here by hand, not by the browser. Sizes up to 64 are stored as 32-bit BMP frames — which store their rows bottom-up and their channels as blue-green-red-alpha, the two details that make hand-rolled ICO files come out upside down or with the red and blue swapped. Sizes of 128 and above are stored as embedded PNG, which every browser and every Windows since Vista reads, and which keeps the file from ballooning: one 256×256 BMP frame alone would be 256 KB.
